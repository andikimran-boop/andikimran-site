HDB to new launch: the upgrade timeline and payment schedule

Book, stay in your flat while it's built, then sell at the right moment. Here's every payment and deadline along the way.

By Andik Imran, CEA R061801FLast reviewed 24 November 2026
Short answerMost HDB upgraders book the new launch first and stay in their flat during construction, which typically takes 3–4 years. You pay 5% at booking, 15% within 8 weeks, and the rest progressively as the building goes up. Singapore Citizen couples pay 20% ABSD upfront, refundable if the HDB is sold within 6 months of TOP or CSC, whichever is earlier.

New launches suit HDB upgraders because you don't have to move twice: you live in your flat until your new home is ready. The catch is cash flow, both the ABSD upfront and carrying two homes for a while.

SSD on new launches

For homes bought from 4 July 2025, selling within 4 years of purchase attracts SSD of up to 16%. The clock starts when you buy, so if you're thinking of selling soon after TOP, check the dates first.

Plan the HDB sale backwards from TOP: list a few months before TOP so your HDB completes inside the 6-month window. Miss it and the ABSD stays with the taxman. I track this date for every client.

Questions families ask me

Can I stay in my HDB while the condo is being built?

Yes. That's the main reason families buy first. But as a Singapore Citizen couple, you pay 20% ABSD upfront on the new launch, and claim it back if you sell the HDB within 6 months of the condo's TOP or CSC, whichever is earlier.

When do the monthly instalments start?

The bank loan is drawn down as construction progresses, so instalments start small after the early stages and grow until TOP and CSC. Make sure you can carry your HDB loan and the rising condo instalments together.

Do I pay an agent fee for a new launch?

No. For new launch projects, the developer pays the agent's fee.

What happens if I don't exercise after booking?

If you don't exercise the Option, the developer can keep a quarter of the 5% booking fee and refunds the rest. Do your sums before you book, not after.

When does the SSD clock start for a new launch?

From when you buy, not when you collect the keys. A 4-year SSD window from booking largely overlaps the construction period.
