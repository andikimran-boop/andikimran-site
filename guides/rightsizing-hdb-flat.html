Rightsizing your HDB when the kids have moved out

How to free up cash, cut the upkeep and stay close to family, without moving twice.

By Andik Imran, CEA R061801FLast reviewed 24 November 2026
Short answerRightsizing means selling a bigger home and moving to a smaller one to free up cash and cut upkeep. Before you sell, check three things: what you'll actually keep after CPF refunds, where you'll live while you move, and whether schemes such as the Silver Housing Bonus apply to you.

The kids have grown up and moved out. The 5-room flat that was once too small now feels too big, and too much to clean. Rightsizing can be a great move, but it's also leaving the family home, so let's do it properly.

Your options

  • A smaller resale flat near your children, your mosque or your clinic.
  • A 2-room Flexi flat on a shorter lease, designed for seniors.
  • A Community Care Apartment, with care services built in.
  • Staying put and unlocking value through HDB schemes, if moving isn't right.

Getting the numbers right

Your sale price minus any loan, minus the CPF refund with accrued interest, gives the cash you'll receive. The CPF refund goes back to your CPF account, which is good for retirement but isn't spending money. I'll show you both figures clearly.

Schemes worth checking

Silver Housing Bonus: eligible seniors who rightsize to a 3-room or smaller flat and top up their CPF Retirement Account may receive a cash bonus. Conditions apply.

Coming from a condo? The 15-month wait-out period for private property owners buying a resale HDB flat was removed in July 2026 for buyers not taking an HDB loan. That makes condo-to-HDB rightsizing much simpler than before.

Move once, not twice

Most rightsizers want to avoid renting in between. We plan the sale and the purchase in parallel and, if needed, arrange a temporary extension of stay with your buyer so you can move straight into the next home.

The part that isn't about money

This is often the home where the kids grew up. Talk it through as a family before you list, and involve the children if they'll be helping with the move. A decision everyone understands is much easier to live with.

How I help: one sit-down with the whole family to lay out the numbers and options, then a plan that moves at your pace. Malay or English, whichever is comfortable.

Questions families ask me

Will we really free up much cash?

Often yes, but less than the headline sale price suggests. CPF refunds go back to your CPF account first. I'll show you the cash and CPF figures separately so there are no surprises.

What is the Silver Housing Bonus?

A cash bonus for eligible older Singaporeans who rightsize to a 3-room or smaller flat and use part of the sale proceeds to top up their CPF Retirement Account. Conditions apply, so check your eligibility with CPF before you plan around it.

We own a condo. Can we downsize to an HDB resale flat?

Yes. The 15-month wait-out period for private property owners buying a resale HDB flat was removed in July 2026 for buyers who don't take an HDB loan. Check the current conditions for your case.

Can we stay in our flat while we look for the next one?

Yes. We can plan the sale and purchase in parallel, and arrange an extension of stay with your buyer if needed, so you only move once.

Is rightsizing the only option?

No. Some families stay put and unlock cash through HDB schemes, rent out a room, or simply wait. We'll look at all of them before deciding.
