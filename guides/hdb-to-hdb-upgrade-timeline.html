HDB to HDB: the upgrade timeline and payment schedule

How to sell your flat and buy the next one in parallel, so you move once and your sale funds the purchase.

By Andik Imran, CEA R061801FLast reviewed 24 November 2026
Short answerPlan for about 3 to 5 months from listing to moving, depending on how fast your flat sells. The usual approach is to sell and buy in parallel, with both completions about 8 weeks after HDB accepts the resale applications, so your sale proceeds and CPF refund fund the next flat.

Moving from one HDB flat to another (bigger, nearer parents, nearer school) is the most common upgrade I handle. The rules are clear; the trick is timing two transactions so you move once.

The timeline

  1. Before you start

    Check MOP, get your HFE letter and run the numbers

    Confirm your MOP date, apply for the HFE letter for the next flat (up to a month), and know your sale proceeds and loan before listing.

  2. Week 0

    SellingRegister your Intent to Sell

    You must register it at least 7 days before you can grant an Option to a buyer.

  3. Weeks 1–6 (varies)

    Pricing right is what makes this quick.

  4. Same weeks

    BuyingShortlist and negotiate your next flat

    Ideally you've agreed terms on your next flat around the time your own sale is agreed.

  5. Option date

    SellingGrant an Option to your buyer; get an Option on your next flat

    Each Option lasts 21 calendar days.

  6. Within 21 days

    BuyingBoth Options exercised

    The buyer exercises on your flat; you exercise on your next flat.

    You pay: exercise fee (deposit up to $5,000 total) · cash

  7. Within 14 days of exercising

    BuyingStamp duty on your purchase

    Buyer's Stamp Duty

  8. Next

    Submit both resale applications

    HDB reviews and accepts them. We align both so the completion dates sit together.

  9. About 8 weeks after HDB's acceptance

    Completion of both: sell, then buy

    Your loan is paid off, your CPF is refunded with interest, the cash comes to you, and the purchase is paid with CPF, cash and the new loan.

Where families get caught: forgetting that the CPF refund goes back to CPF, not to cash. If your next flat needs more cash than you think, the deal can wobble at the last step. We check this before you list.

Questions families ask me

Can I buy the new flat before selling my current one?

Most families sell and buy in parallel, timing both completions close together. HDB does allow some owners to buy first and sell within a set period, subject to conditions. We'll check what applies to you.

Where does my sale money go?

At completion, your outstanding loan is paid off and the CPF you used (plus accrued interest) is refunded to your CPF account. The cash balance comes to you. Both can fund your next flat.

What if my new flat isn't ready when I sell?

You can ask your buyer for a temporary extension of stay after completion. I usually agree this upfront in the deal so nobody is surprised.

Do I pay ABSD buying another HDB flat?

No. ABSD isn't payable on HDB flats when a Singapore Citizen is among the buyers, because HDB rules already require the existing flat to be sold.
