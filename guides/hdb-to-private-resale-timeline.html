HDB to private resale: the upgrade timeline and payment schedule

The two ways to sequence it, what each costs upfront, and the timing that keeps ABSD off your bill.

By Andik Imran, CEA R061801FLast reviewed 24 November 2026
Short answerThe usual path takes about 4 to 6 months. Most families sell the HDB first and time the condo purchase so it completes after the HDB does, avoiding ABSD up front. The other path is buying first: you pay 20% ABSD on the condo, then claim it back if the HDB is sold within 6 months.

Going from HDB to a resale condo means juggling two different systems: HDB's resale process and the private-property process. The order you do them in decides how much cash you need upfront.

If you sell the condo early: SSD

Plan to hold for at least 4 years. Selling a private home bought from 4 July 2025 within 4 years attracts Seller's Stamp Duty:

1 year16%12%

After 4 yearsNo SSDNo SSD
The timing trick: the dates in the condo Option are negotiable. Getting the exercise date to fall after your HDB completes is often the difference between needing $0 or $200,000+ in ABSD cash. Agree this before you sign anything.

Questions families ask me

Do I have to sell my HDB before buying a condo?

No. After MOP you can buy private property while keeping your HDB flat. But as a Singapore Citizen couple, the condo would be your second property, so ABSD applies up front.

How does the ABSD refund work?

A married couple, with at least one Singapore Citizen, buying the condo jointly can claim back the ABSD if they sell their HDB within 6 months of buying a completed property. You must pay the ABSD first, and the refund isn't automatic. Your lawyer applies for it.

Can I use an HDB loan for a condo?

No. Private property uses a bank loan. The TDSR limit (55% of income for all debts) applies, but not the 30% MSR that applies to HDB flats.

What is SSD and does it affect me?

Seller's Stamp Duty applies if you sell the condo within 4 years of buying it (for purchases from 4 July 2025). It doesn't affect selling your HDB, because the MOP is longer. See my SSD guide.
