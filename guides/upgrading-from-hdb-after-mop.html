Upgrading from your HDB after MOP: sell first or buy first?

What your sale really gives you, what you can safely borrow, and the order of moves that keeps your family comfortable.

By Andik Imran, CEA R061801FLast reviewed 24 November 2026
Short answerMost HDB upgraders sell first, or sell and buy in parallel, so the sale proceeds fund the next home and you avoid paying ABSD up front on a private property. Buying first can work, but only if the numbers still hold when your sale takes longer than you hoped.

You've hit MOP, the kids are bigger, and every weekend you're walking through showflats. Before you fall in love with a floor plan, here's how I walk families through the decision.

Are you past MOP?

You can sell on the open market once you've met the Minimum Occupation Period: 5 years for most flats, and 10 years for Plus and Prime flats. Your exact date is on My HDBPage.

What your sale actually gives you

A simple way to see it:

Sale price − outstanding loan − CPF refund (with accrued interest) = cash in hand

The CPF refund isn't lost. It goes back to your CPF Ordinary Account and can be used for your next home. But it isn't cash, and many families overestimate what they'll have in the bank.

How much can you borrow?

Bank loans are limited by your income and existing debts, your age and the loan tenure. For HDB flats and ECs there's an extra cap on how much of your income can go to the mortgage. The bank's maximum is a ceiling, not a target. That's what the SAFE plan is for.

Sell first or buy first?

ApproachGood forWatch out for
Sell firstKnowing your exact budget; no ABSD on a private purchaseWhere you'll live in between

Buy firstNew launches with a long build timeABSD paid up front on private property; refund only if you sell in time

Costs families forget

  • Stamp duty and legal fees on the purchase
  • Renovation and furniture (usually more than planned)
  • Moving, and rent if there's a gap
  • Higher maintenance fees in a condo

The SAFE check

Before you sell, we check that the Sale pays for the move, money is set Aside for emergencies, Four years of the mortgage are covered, and it all holds Even if rates rise. If it passes, upgrade with confidence. If not, we adjust the plan.

My honest advice: the families who are happiest a year later didn't buy the biggest home they could. They bought the one that still left room to breathe.

Questions families ask me

When can I sell my BTO?

After the Minimum Occupation Period. That's 5 years for most flats. Plus and Prime flats have a 10-year MOP. You can check your exact MOP date on My HDBPage.

Why does my CPF refund matter so much?

When you sell, the CPF you used for the flat, plus the interest it would have earned, goes back to your CPF account first. That reduces the cash you receive, but you can use the refunded CPF for your next home. Many families forget the accrued interest and overestimate their cash.

Can I stay in my flat after it's sold?

Yes, if your buyer agrees, you can arrange a temporary extension of stay after completion. It's a common way to bridge the gap while your next home is being finished or handed over.

Should I buy near my parents?

If family support matters to you, it's worth it, and there's a grant for buying a resale HDB flat near or with your parents. I'll check whether you qualify.

Is now a good time to upgrade?

The better question is whether your numbers pass SAFE today. If they do, timing the market matters less. If they don't, waiting may be the smart move.
