How I plan every upgrade

The SAFE Upgrading Plan

A nicer home that keeps you up at night isn't an upgrade. Before you sell, we check four things.

S

Sale pays for it

Your sale proceeds cover the downpayment and costs. No digging into the family's savings to make it work.

A

Aside first

An emergency fund comes out of the sale before we decide how much home you buy.

F

Four years covered

Four years of the new mortgage covered by CPF and the cash buffer from your sale, not your salary.

E

Even if rates rise

We test your repayments at higher interest rates, so a rate hike is an inconvenience, not a crisis.

How the SAFE plan works

A planning framework, not a guarantee. Your numbers depend on your sale price, loan and CPF.

Questions I get every week

Do I have to commit to selling before we talk?

No. Plenty of first chats end with “not yet”, and that's a good outcome. The Sale Check and our first sit-down are free, with no obligation to list.

Who do you work with?

Pretty much everyone looking to buy, sell or rent a home in Singapore: first-timers, young families moving up, older couples rightsizing, landlords and investors. If I'm not the right person for something, I'll tell you and point you to someone who is.

Can we speak in Malay?

Boleh. I'm comfortable in Malay and English, and so is Mirta. Many families switch between both in the same meeting, and that's perfectly fine. More on my Malay property agent page.

Do you also help with new launch condos?

Yes. If your next home is a new condo or EC, I plan the sale and the purchase together so the timing and cash flow work. See the new launch page.

What counts as a “complicated” case?

Inherited flats and faraid, estates still waiting on paperwork, divorce, tight sale-and-buy timelines, and flats needing an extension of stay. These are the cases I enjoy most, because a calm plan makes the biggest difference.

Is Homes with AIMS a different agency?

No. Homes with AIMS is my wife Mirta and me working as a team, both with PropNex. This site is my own page. homeswithaims.com is our team's.
