From HDB to a new launch, in the right order.

A new condo or EC is exciting. The showflat is designed to be. My job is the part after the excitement: making sure your HDB sale, CPF, loan and the developer's payment schedule all line up.

The three questions that matter more than the floor plan

Can we afford it in year three, not just today?

Progressive payments start small and rise as the project is built. We plan for the payments you'll face at TOP, not just the first cheque.

What's the order of moves?

Sell first, buy first, or both in parallel. Each has a different cash and ABSD picture. We choose deliberately.

Where will we live in between?

If your new home isn't ready when your HDB sale completes, you'll need a bridge: extension of stay, family, or renting. We plan it before you sign.

New launch questions

Do I pay you a fee to buy a new launch?

For new launch projects, the developer pays the agent's fee. You get my help comparing projects, planning finances and handling the booking at no cost to you.

Can I just walk into the showflat on my own?

You can, but you'll only hear about one project from the people selling it. I'll help you compare a few side by side and check the numbers against your HDB sale first.

Should I sell my HDB first?

Usually, or at least plan both together. If you buy private property before selling your HDB, ABSD may apply up front. Married couples can apply for a refund if the HDB is sold within the allowed period, but you need the cash to pay it first. We map this out before you book.

EC or condo?

It depends on your household income, eligibility and how long you plan to stay. ECs come with their own rules and minimum occupation period, so we look at both against your SAFE numbers.

General information only. ABSD, loan and EC eligibility rules change; we check yours against current IRAS, HDB and MAS rules.
