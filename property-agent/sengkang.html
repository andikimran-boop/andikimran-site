What makes Sengkang, Sengkang

Sengkang is a well-planned family town where homes, LRT and amenities were built together. Each of its 'vale' neighbourhoods has its own feel.

Planned from day one

Sengkang was one of the first new towns built together with its transport links. The LRT loops reach almost every precinct.

A hospital in town

Sengkang General Hospital and Sengkang Community Hospital are right here, a real comfort for families with young kids or elderly parents.

Riverside green

Sengkang Riverside Park covers 21 hectares, with mangrove and civic areas, and connects to Punggol and the Round Island Route park connectors.

Four 'vales', four characters

Rivervale and Compassvale near the town centre, Anchorvale by the river, and Fernvale on the west side near The Seletar Mall.

Easy to the airport

Bus service 110 links Compassvale and Anchorvale to all Changi Airport terminals, handy for aviation and airport workers.

Better links east

The Cross Island Line Punggol Extension, targeted for 2032, will give the north-east a direct rail link to Pasir Ris and Tampines North.

Who Sengkang suits

  • Families who want a mature-feeling town with a hospital close by
  • People working at Seletar, the airport or in the north-east
  • Upgraders who want to stay near family already in Sengkang or Punggol
  • Buyers who like a quieter, park-connected neighbourhood

What's coming

  • Cross Island Line Punggol Extension (targeted 2032), improving links to Pasir Ris and Tampines North
  • Continued upgrades to park connectors and riverside spaces
  • More flats reaching MOP, giving buyers more choice

Selling in Sengkang: what buyers look at

  • Which LRT station you're near, and whether it's on the East or West loop, matters a lot to buyers.
  • Distance to Sengkang or Buangkok MRT and to the malls is the next thing they compare.
  • Buyers often compare Sengkang with Punggol, so we price against both, not just your street.
  • Families check primary schools nearby. We highlight them in the listing.

How to choose the best property agent in Sengkang

"Best" means different things to different families. Whoever you speak to, me included, here's what I'd check:

  • Ask what they've sold nearby, and for how much. Recent, real transactions near your block tell you more than any slogan. Ask how they priced them and how long they took.
  • Check their reviews and CEA registration. Look for recent Google reviews from real clients, and check the agent's name on the CEA Public Register.
  • Ask them to explain your numbers. Not just the selling price: your CPF refund, cash proceeds and timeline. If they can't explain it simply, keep looking.
  • Be careful with the highest valuation. An exciting number can be a way to win your listing. Ask what it's based on.
  • Ask how they'll market your home. Photos, video, portals, social media, and who will actually be at the viewings.
  • Pick someone you can talk to. You'll be messaging this person for months. Make sure they reply, explain, and don't pressure you.
