What makes Pasir Ris, Pasir Ris

Pasir Ris, 'white sands' in Malay, is the east's seaside town: relaxed, green, and home to some of the roomiest flats around.

A beach in your backyard

Pasir Ris Park stretches along the coast, with cycling paths, mangroves and weekend barbecues. Downtown East is next door for the kids.

A refreshed town centre

The Pasir Ris Integrated Transport Hub has opened, with Pasir Ris Mall, a polyclinic, childcare and an air-conditioned bus interchange.

Space for big families

Pasir Ris is known for larger, older flats, popular with multi-generation families and anyone who needs room.

A future interchange

Pasir Ris station is set to become an interchange with the Cross Island Line, targeted for 2030, plus a new Pasir Ris East station.

Punggol, one line away

The Cross Island Line Punggol Extension, targeted for 2032, will link Pasir Ris to Punggol in about 15 minutes.

Quiet, mature, green

A mature town being rejuvenated under HDB's Remaking Our Heartland programme, without losing its calm.

Selling in Pasir Ris: what buyers look at

  • For bigger, older flats, buyers weigh space against remaining lease. The lease affects their CPF and loan, so we price with that in mind.
  • Many buyers plan to renovate. Good photos of the space and a realistic price matter more than staging.
  • Distance to Pasir Ris MRT and the new mall is a strong selling point.
  • Multi-generation buyers care about layouts with separate living spaces, so we highlight that clearly.

How to choose the best property agent in Pasir Ris

"Best" means different things to different families. Whoever you speak to, me included, here's what I'd check:

  • Ask what they've sold nearby, and for how much. Recent, real transactions near your block tell you more than any slogan. Ask how they priced them and how long they took.
  • Check their reviews and CEA registration. Look for recent Google reviews from real clients, and check the agent's name on the CEA Public Register.
  • Ask them to explain your numbers. Not just the selling price: your CPF refund, cash proceeds and timeline. If they can't explain it simply, keep looking.
  • Be careful with the highest valuation. An exciting number can be a way to win your listing. Ask what it's based on.
  • Ask how they'll market your home. Photos, video, portals, social media, and who will actually be at the viewings.
  • Pick someone you can talk to. You'll be messaging this person for months. Make sure they reply, explain, and don't pressure you.
