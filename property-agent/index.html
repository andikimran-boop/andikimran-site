Estate guides: what makes each town special

I help clients all over Singapore. Here's what I've learned about some of the estates I'm asked about most: who they suit, what's coming, and what buyers look for.

Estate guide

Yishun

Yishun is one of the north's original new towns, with development starting in 1981. It's big, established and still growing, mixing older blocks with brand-new neighbourhoods.

Read about Yishun →

Estate guide

Punggol

Punggol is Singapore's waterfront town: young, family-heavy and changing fast, with a growing digital business district.

Read about Punggol →

Estate guide

Sengkang

Sengkang is a well-planned family town where homes, LRT and amenities were built together. Each of its 'vale' neighbourhoods has its own feel.

Read about Sengkang →

Estate guide

Tampines

Tampines is the east's big, self-contained town. It's Singapore's first regional centre, so you rarely need to leave it for anything.

Read about Tampines →

Estate guide

Pasir Ris

Pasir Ris, 'white sands' in Malay, is the east's seaside town: relaxed, green, and home to some of the roomiest flats around.
