What makes Tampines, Tampines

Tampines is the east's big, self-contained town. It's Singapore's first regional centre, so you rarely need to leave it for anything.

A regional centre

Tampines was planned as Singapore's first regional centre, with offices, three malls and services clustered around the MRT.

Our Tampines Hub

A huge community and lifestyle hub with a stadium, pools, library, hawker centre and government services under one roof.

Two train lines, a third coming

East-West Line and Downtown Line stations today, with the Cross Island Line at Tampines North targeted for 2030.

Work nearby

Changi Business Park, the airport and the east's industrial estates are close, so many residents have short commutes.

Tampines North is growing

A newer neighbourhood with its own upcoming MRT station and integrated transport hub.

Green corners

Tampines Eco Green and the park connectors offer quieter spaces inside a busy town.

Who Tampines suits

  • Families who want everything in one town: malls, sports, schools, transport

  • Upgraders staying in the east to keep kids in the same schools

Selling in Tampines: what buyers look at

  • Tampines is big, so micro-location decides price: which MRT line, which mall, which school.
  • Downtown Line access (Tampines West, Tampines East) is a selling point for buyers working in town.
  • Families focus on primary schools within reach. We put that front and centre.
  • Older blocks near the town centre compete on location; newer Tampines North flats compete on condition.

How to choose the best property agent in Tampines

"Best" means different things to different families. Whoever you speak to, me included, here's what I'd check:

  • Ask what they've sold nearby, and for how much. Recent, real transactions near your block tell you more than any slogan. Ask how they priced them and how long they took.
  • Check their reviews and CEA registration. Look for recent Google reviews from real clients, and check the agent's name on the CEA Public Register.
  • Ask them to explain your numbers. Not just the selling price: your CPF refund, cash proceeds and timeline. If they can't explain it simply, keep looking.
  • Be careful with the highest valuation. An exciting number can be a way to win your listing. Ask what it's based on.
  • Ask how they'll market your home. Photos, video, portals, social media, and who will actually be at the viewings.
  • Pick someone you can talk to. You'll be messaging this person for months. Make sure they reply, explain, and don't pressure you.
