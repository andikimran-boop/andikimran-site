The SAFE Upgrading Plan

How I help families upgrade without stretching. Four checks we run before you sell, so your next home is one you can enjoy, not one you worry about.

S

Sale pays for it

The downpayment, stamp duty, legal fees and moving costs come from your sale proceeds and CPF. If the plan only works by draining your savings, it's not the plan.

A

Aside first

Before we set a budget, an emergency fund is ring-fenced from the sale. That money is for life, not for the house.

F

Four years covered

Four years of the new monthly instalment, covered by your CPF and the cash buffer from your sale rather than your salary.

E

Even if rates rise

We test your repayments at higher interest rates than today's. If it still holds, you're safe. If not, we adjust before you commit.

What we look at in your sit-down

  • Your sale: a realistic price range from recent transactions in your block and estate, not the highest number on the portal.
  • What comes back to you: outstanding loan, the CPF refund (principal plus accrued interest) and the cash you'll actually receive.
  • What you can borrow: loan limits based on income, age and existing debts, and what that means for your budget.
  • Your buffer: the emergency fund and the four-year cover, set aside before the budget.
  • Your timeline: sell first, buy first, or both together, and whether you'll need an extension of stay.
  • Your life: schools, parents nearby, commute, the next five years.

You leave with a one-page plan: the budget that passes SAFE, the timeline, and what has to be true for it to work.

Common questions

Why four years of mortgage covered?

Four years is long enough to ride out most rough patches: a job change, a new baby, one partner taking a break, a rate cycle. It means your new home isn't relying on everything going right from day one.

Isn't it better to borrow the maximum the bank allows?

The bank's limit tells you what you're allowed to borrow, not what's comfortable. Plenty of families qualify for more than they should take. SAFE is how we find the comfortable number.

What if my numbers don't pass SAFE?

Then I'll tell you, and we look at the options: a different budget, a different timeline, waiting a year, or staying put. I'd rather lose a listing than put a family under pressure.

Does SAFE apply to new launch condos too?

Yes. With a new launch, we also map the progressive payment schedule against your sale and CPF timeline so there's no gap.

SAFE is a planning framework, not financial advice or a guarantee. For loans, I'll connect you with a mortgage banker who can confirm your figures.
