Questions

Do you only work with Malay clients?

Not at all. I work with families of every background. Many of my clients are Malay families, and I'm glad to explain everything in Malay when that's more comfortable.

Boleh bincang dalam Bahasa Melayu?

Boleh. Saya selesa berbincang dalam Bahasa Melayu atau English, dan ramai keluarga guna kedua-duanya dalam satu perjumpaan.

Can you help with a flat under faraid?

Yes. I work alongside your lawyer or the Syariah Court process on the property side: valuation, family agreement on price and timeline, and the HDB sale. My faraid guide explains the steps.

Can my parents join the meeting?

Please do. Many families decide together with Mak and Abah, so I'm happy to meet at home and explain the numbers to everyone in the language they're comfortable with.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Singapore. I know Punggol, Sengkang, Tampines, Pasir Ris and Yishun especially well.
