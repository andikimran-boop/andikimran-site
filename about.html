Hi, I'm Andik.

Husband to Mirta, dad of four, and a property consultant with PropNex since 2019. I help people buy, sell and rent homes across Singapore, and I like to get the numbers sorted before anything else.

Say hi on WhatsApp

Why I do this

Our first home was a 3-room BTO. When the family grew, Mirta and I upgraded to a condo, and I learned first-hand how different the decision feels from the inside. The question was never “can the bank lend us this?” It was “will we still sleep well in three years if something goes wrong?”

That question became the SAFE Upgrading Plan. Every family I work with sees their sale proceeds, CPF refund, loan limits and buffer on paper before we talk about listing. Some decide to upgrade. Some decide to wait. Both are good outcomes if they're the right ones.

Who I work with

All sorts of people, all over Singapore: first-time buyers, young families moving up, older couples rightsizing, landlords and investors. Many of my clients are Malay families, and I'm always happy to chat in Malay. I also help families dealing with an inherited flat, faraid or a divorce, where a calm, organised consultant makes a real difference.

What I won't do

  • Quote you an exciting price just to win your listing.
  • Push you to sell when the numbers say wait.
  • Leave you guessing. You'll know what's happening and what comes next.
