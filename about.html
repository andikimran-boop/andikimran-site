In short

I'm a full-time property consultant with PropNex, focused on HDB resale, upgrading and new launches. Before property I studied business and worked in finance, which is why every client of mine sees their sale proceeds, CPF refund, loan limits and buffer on paper first. That approach became my SAFE Upgrading Plan.

Most of my work comes from referrals and from families who find me on Google. Many are young couples upgrading from their first BTO, older couples rightsizing, and families handling the hard cases: an inherited flat under faraid, an estate, or a divorce. I handle every client personally, from the first sit-down to key handover.

What I'm good at

The skills you're really hiring when you appoint an agent.

Pricing and negotiation

Pricing from real transactions in your block, not portal wishlists, then negotiating firmly for you. One seller whose flat sat unsold for months with another agent had it sold within weeks, and a new buyer within 48 hours when the first one pulled out.

Numbers and planning

CPF refunds, accrued interest, loan limits, TDSR and MSR, stamp duties, and the order of moves. I put it all on one page so you can decide calmly. Try my loan calculator to see how I think.

The complicated cases

Faraid and inherited flats, estates, divorce, tight sell-and-buy timelines. I once helped a client challenge an IRAS stamp-duty over-assessment with an accredited valuer. Calm and organised is the job.

Marketing that sells homes

Listing photos and video, property portals, social media, and Google and Meta ads that I run and train other agents on. Your home gets seen by the right buyers fast.

Systems that keep you informed

I build my own tools: an automated property report that reaches your WhatsApp in minutes, a lead tracker so no buyer enquiry is missed, and clear updates at every step.

Explaining things clearly

English or Malay, to you and to your parents. As a former Toastmasters club president who has competed in speech contests, I'm at home in front of a family or a room.
